03  —  SOLUTION
Solution Overview
Solution Summary

DoseWISE is a web platform that connects patients, doctors and family caregivers around one shared, versioned medication record.

Doctors prescribe and edit with full history and automatic safety checks. Patients get reminders and log every dose. Caregivers see only what the patient allows. In an emergency, any doctor can open a read-only chart using the patient’s Access ID.

Built with React + FastAPI and running on AWS in Mumbai (ap-south-1).

Core Value Proposition

One trustworthy medication record that follows the patient.

  • Every dose logged: taken, late or missed
  • Every prescription change versioned and safety-checked
  • Every access consented, time-limited and audited
Key Features
01

Versioned e-prescriptions, safety-checked for duplicates, allergies and Bedrock AI screening

02

Reminders and an honest dose log (taken / late / missed) the doctor sees live

03

Patient Access ID: 90-day doctor access or a one-time guest emergency view

Target Users
  • Adults on long-term medicines from several doctors (diabetes, hypertension, elderly care)
  • Family caregivers who help manage medicines
  • Doctors, clinics and emergency physicians in India

04  —  PROBLEM
Problem Validation & Approach
EXISTING GAP
PROPOSED APPROACH
Problem Context

People with chronic conditions take several medicines prescribed by different doctors. Prescriptions live on paper slips and in separate hospital systems, so no one sees the full, current list.

The WHO estimates that only about 50% of patients with chronic disease take their medicines as prescribed (WHO, 2003). Our pilot would measure our own baseline.

Current Limitations
  • No shared, versioned medication record across doctors
  • Missed doses go unnoticed until the next visit
  • Emergency doctors can’t see current medicines or allergies
  • Reminder apps aren’t linked to the prescriber or the caregiver
Proposed Approach

DoseWISE makes the prescription the single source of truth and connects everyone to it:

  • Doctors issue versioned prescriptions; every change is checked for duplicate therapy, documented allergies and AI-screened drug interactions (Amazon Bedrock, shown as unverified)
  • Patients get reminders and log each dose; late (>60 min) and missed doses reach the doctor and caregiver
  • Consent first: Access ID for doctors, scoped caregiver access and a read-only emergency view, all audited
  • Built on AWS in Mumbai: Cognito, DynamoDB, Bedrock, SNS, Location Service, S3 + KMS
